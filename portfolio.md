## Projects

### 1. Appnea: Breathe Better, Sleep Smarter

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Date Creation | May/2025 |
| Project Type | National Competition |
| Duration | 2 Months |
| My Role | Product Manager, UI/UX Designer |
| Link | Prototype |

**Description:** Sleep apnea is a serious sleep disorder that affects sleep quality and increases the risk of long-term health problems, yet many people remain unaware of their condition due to limited awareness and early detection access. Appnea was created as an AI-powered sleep monitoring application that analyzes breathing sounds and patterns through a smartphone to help users identify potential sleep apnea risks. By providing an affordable, practical, and accessible solution without requiring additional medical devices, Appnea aims to make sleep health monitoring easier for the wider community.

**Vision:** Appnea envisions a future where people can take control of their sleep health through accessible and innovative technology. By combining artificial intelligence, sleep monitoring, and user-centered design, Appnea aims to encourage early awareness, support healthier sleep habits, and promote preventive healthcare. This initiative aligns with Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 3 by contributing to improved health and well-being through digital health innovation.

**Goals:** The goal of Appnea is to provide users with an easy-to-use platform that helps detect potential sleep apnea risks, increase awareness of the importance of sleep quality, and support early prevention of sleep-related health issues. Through AI-based analysis and personalized sleep insights, Appnea aims to reduce undiagnosed sleep apnea cases while creating a more inclusive and accessible approach to maintaining long-term health.

**Journey:** Sleep apnea is often a "silent threat" because many people experience its symptoms without realizing the impact on their health. Fatigue, difficulty concentrating, and increased health risks can occur simply because sleep quality is disrupted. Through research and interviews with sleep apnea patients, individuals with related symptoms, and healthcare professionals, we discovered that early detection remains a challenge. Many users struggle to recognize their symptoms, find existing monitoring methods uncomfortable and expensive, and lack access to a simple solution that helps them understand their sleep condition. From these insights, Appnea was developed with an empathetic approach, not only focusing on sleep apnea as a medical issue, but also understanding how it affects daily activities, productivity, and quality of life. Appnea aims to provide a non-invasive, intuitive, and accessible way for users to monitor their sleep and become more aware of potential risks.

**Accessibility and Design Approach:** Appnea is designed to make sleep health monitoring more accessible for a wider range of users. The application focuses on simplicity, comfort, and usability by allowing users to monitor their sleep through smartphone-based sound and breathing analysis without requiring additional medical equipment. The interface is designed with clear information, easy navigation, and understandable insights so users can recognize their sleep patterns without needing medical expertise. The visual identity also supports this approach through the use of a moon icon and purple gradient color. The moon represents nighttime, rest, and the natural sleep cycle, while the purple tone reflects calmness, relaxation, health, and innovation. This combination creates a sense of comfort and trust, aligning with Appnea's purpose of helping users achieve better sleep through intelligent technology.

**Hope:** Through Appnea, we hope to create a future where people are more aware of their sleep health and able to take preventive action earlier. By providing a practical and affordable sleep monitoring solution, Appnea aims to reduce the number of undiagnosed sleep apnea cases and encourage healthier lifestyles. Beyond being a technology product, Appnea represents a movement toward making healthcare more inclusive, where everyone has the opportunity to understand their body, improve their quality of sleep, and achieve better overall well-being.

**Design System:** Typeface Plus Jakarta Sans. Primary palette in a blue-purple scale (blue-50 to blue-900).

**Related achievement:** 3rd Place UX Competition FindIT UGM 2025.

### 2. LiboorIn: Enhance the safety of your travel experience

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Date Creation | Aug/2024 |
| Project Type | National Competition |
| Duration | 1 Month |
| Role | Product Manager |
| Link | Prototype |

**Description:** LiboorIn is a travel platform designed to help travelers discover and experience local destinations in Indonesia more easily. Based on user research and interviews, we found that many travelers face challenges such as unclear pricing, limited information, difficult access, and a lack of trusted guidance when exploring local destinations. LiboorIn addresses these problems by providing an integrated platform that combines destination recommendations, local tour guide services, and ticket booking to create a more convenient, authentic, and transparent travel experience while supporting local tourism growth.

**Vision:** LiboorIn envisions a future where every traveler can explore Indonesia's local destinations in a more meaningful, sustainable, and responsible way. By connecting travelers with local communities and authentic cultural experiences, LiboorIn aims to create a balance between tourism development, environmental preservation, and local economic empowerment. The platform encourages travelers to discover hidden destinations while maintaining comfort, safety, and respect for local values.

**Goals:** LiboorIn aims to simplify the travel planning process by providing reliable information, transparent pricing, and accessible services in one platform. Through personalized recommendations and local community involvement, LiboorIn seeks to improve the quality of travel experiences, reduce the impact of overtourism, and support sustainable tourism by helping travelers make more informed and responsible choices.

**Journey:** Indonesia has a rich diversity of natural landscapes and local cultures that attract travelers from around the world. However, through user research and interviews with local tourists, international travelers, destination managers, and tourism stakeholders, we discovered several challenges that affect travel experiences. Many travelers struggle with unclear pricing, limited destination information, difficult access, safety concerns, and the impact of overtourism that reduces the authenticity of local experiences. On the other hand, local businesses also face challenges such as competition with larger platforms, complicated regulations, and difficulties in promoting their services. From these insights, LiboorIn was created as a solution that connects travelers with authentic local experiences while supporting local communities. LiboorIn focuses on understanding both traveler needs and local tourism challenges to create a more meaningful and sustainable travel ecosystem.

**Accessibility and Design Approach:** LiboorIn is designed to make travel planning more accessible, transparent, and user-friendly for everyone. The platform provides integrated features such as destination recommendations, local guide services, and ticket booking to reduce uncertainty during the travel journey. The design approach focuses on simplicity, trust, and comfort by presenting clear information, transparent pricing, and reliable recommendations that help users make better travel decisions. The visual identity uses a modern blue-based color palette combined with clean typography to represent trust, exploration, professionalism, and freedom. Blue reflects the feeling of calmness and reliability, creating a digital environment where users can confidently discover new destinations while maintaining a connection with authentic local experiences.

**Hope:** Through LiboorIn, we hope to create a future where tourism can grow sustainably while preserving local culture, nature, and community values. LiboorIn aims to empower travelers to explore Indonesia responsibly, support local businesses, and discover destinations beyond overcrowded tourist areas. More than just a travel platform, LiboorIn represents a bridge between travelers and local communities, creating opportunities for meaningful experiences while contributing to a healthier and more sustainable tourism ecosystem.

**Design System:** Typeface Nunito Sans (Regular, Medium, Semi-Bold, Bold, ExtraBold). Color palette: #0059C8, #D8E9F9, #FFFFFF, #E4E7EC, #1D1F23, #40444D, #D0D5DD, #7A7F89.

**Process:** Design Thinking (Empathize, Define, Ideate, Design, Testing). Research included user personas (Wayan Pambudi, a 45-year-old homestay owner; Hanny Ozora, a 30-year-old Taiwanese traveler) and a competitor analysis.

**Description:** InCareer is a platform designed to help people with physical disabilities find job opportunities that match their skills and abilities through a simple and accessible process: choose, apply, and wait. Based on research, many people with disabilities still face challenges in entering the workforce because companies often have difficulty identifying their potential and suitable roles. As a result, many workers with disabilities are placed in informal jobs that do not fully represent their capabilities. InCareer addresses this gap by connecting job seekers with inclusive companies through skill-based recommendations, streamlined applications, and a platform that highlights each user's strengths.

**Vision:** InCareer envisions an inclusive employment ecosystem where people with disabilities have equal opportunities to showcase their abilities and build meaningful careers. By creating a bridge between companies and talented individuals with disabilities, InCareer aims to change the perception of disability in the workplace from limitation into potential. The platform supports a future where accessibility, diversity, and equal opportunities become essential parts of professional growth.

**Goals:** InCareer aims to simplify the employment process for people with physical disabilities by providing personalized job recommendations, accessible job applications, and tools to showcase their skills and experiences. Through features such as job category matching, company information, application tracking, and disability-friendly recruitment support, InCareer seeks to empower users, help companies discover qualified talent, and contribute to a more inclusive workforce.

**Key Features:**
- **Dashboard:** A real-time online job application process.
- **Recommendations:** Provides job recommendations based on your previous jobs / experiences that match your criteria.
- **Categories:** Provides job category options based on the qualifications listed on your profile.

**Journey:** People with disabilities often face barriers when entering the workforce, not because of a lack of ability, but because their skills and potential are not always recognized by companies. Through research, we found that many job seekers with disabilities struggle to find opportunities that match their abilities, while companies also experience difficulties identifying suitable candidates. This gap causes many people with disabilities to be placed in jobs that do not fully represent their expertise. From these insights, InCareer was developed as a platform that connects people with disabilities and inclusive companies by focusing on skills, qualifications, and accessibility. InCareer simplifies the job search process into three simple steps: choose, apply, and wait, allowing users to focus on their potential rather than their limitations.

**Accessibility:** InCareer is designed to create a more inclusive and accessible job-search experience for people with physical disabilities. The platform focuses on reducing complexity by providing personalized job recommendations, clear company information, and a simple application process that can be completed efficiently. The design approach prioritizes usability, readability, and accessibility by ensuring that users can easily navigate through job categories, manage their profiles, and track their applications. InCareer is developed for Android devices to reach a wider range of users, especially considering accessibility for users who may use different device capabilities. By creating a user-centered experience, InCareer aims to make employment opportunities more reachable and comfortable for everyone.

**Hope:** Through InCareer, we hope to create a future where disability is no longer seen as a barrier to professional growth. The platform aims to empower people with disabilities by helping them showcase their skills, access suitable career opportunities, and build confidence in the workplace. At the same time, InCareer encourages companies to recognize the value of an inclusive workforce and contribute to a more equal employment ecosystem where everyone has the opportunity to succeed.

**Design System:** Logotype in Montserrat Medium, body in Montserrat Regular. Color palette: Blue #0053F5, Dark Powder Blue #00359E, Cyan-Blue #82AAE3. Logo breakdown: Dasi (represents employees/workers), Huruf D (represents diversity), Huruf I (represents inclusivity/information).

### 4. EcoWardrobe: Build a better environment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Date Creation | 09/2024 |
| Project Type | National Competition |
| Duration | <1 Month |
| Role | Product Manager, UI/UX Designer |
| Link | Prototype |

**Description:** EcoWardrobe is a sustainable fashion platform designed to help people reduce fast fashion waste by extending the lifecycle of clothing. The rapid growth of the fashion industry has created a significant environmental impact through excessive production, textile waste, and unsustainable consumption habits. Based on research, many people still own unused clothes but lack accessible solutions to reuse or redistribute them. EcoWardrobe addresses this problem by providing a platform where users can sell, buy, and donate wearable clothing while encouraging more responsible fashion consumption.

**Vision:** EcoWardrobe envisions a future where fashion consumption becomes more responsible, sustainable, and environmentally conscious. By connecting people through a circular fashion ecosystem, EcoWardrobe aims to reduce clothing waste, encourage mindful consumption, and empower communities to participate in protecting the environment. The platform supports Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 12 by promoting responsible consumption and production, creating a movement where fashion is not only about trends but also about long-term positive impact.

**Goals:** EcoWardrobe aims to provide an accessible solution for users to extend the lifespan of their clothing through selling, purchasing, and donating second-hand fashion items. By offering a convenient platform and personalized shopping experience, EcoWardrobe helps users make sustainable choices while reducing the environmental impact of fast fashion. The goal is to build awareness, change consumer behavior, and create a collaborative community that contributes to a more sustainable fashion industry.

**Journey:** The fashion industry continues to grow rapidly, but the rise of fast fashion has created a major environmental challenge through excessive clothing production, waste accumulation, and unsustainable consumption habits. Through research, surveys, and interviews with young fashion consumers, we discovered that many people frequently purchase new clothes to follow trends but often leave unused clothing stored or thrown away. Users expressed that they wanted to reduce their environmental impact but lacked an accessible platform to reuse, sell, donate, or exchange their clothes. From these insights, EcoWardrobe was developed as a solution that transforms unused clothing into valuable resources by creating a circular fashion ecosystem where users can extend the lifecycle of their clothes while contributing to a more sustainable future.

**Accessibility:** EcoWardrobe is designed to make sustainable fashion accessible and convenient for everyone by providing a simple platform to buy, sell, and donate second-hand clothing. The application focuses on creating a user-friendly experience through clear navigation, personalized recommendations, and features that support users in making more responsible fashion choices. With features such as EcoMarket for selling unused clothes, EcoDonate for supporting communities, EcoScan for personalized style recommendations, and EcoLearn for sustainability education, EcoWardrobe combines technology and environmental awareness in one platform. The design approach prioritizes simplicity, inclusivity, and engagement to encourage users from different backgrounds to participate in sustainable fashion practices.

**Hope:** Through EcoWardrobe, we hope to create a future where fashion consumption becomes more responsible and environmentally conscious. By encouraging people to reuse, resell, and donate their clothing, EcoWardrobe aims to reduce fast fashion waste and change the "buy and discard" mindset into a more sustainable lifestyle. Beyond being a marketplace, EcoWardrobe represents a community-driven movement that connects people, supports circular fashion, and contributes to Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 12 by promoting responsible consumption and production.

**Design System:** Typeface Montserrat Regular. Brown palette with a contrast check of #6B240C against #994D1C (contrast 10.7, AAA for normal and large text). Logo elements: Bintang, Baju, Kotak Donasi.

**Related achievement:** 2nd Runner Up UI/UX Competition The Ace Universitas Diponegoro 2024.
