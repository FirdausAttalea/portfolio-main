### 6.1 Appnea | `appnea`

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Tagline | Breathe Better, Sleep Smarter. |

| Project type | National Competition (FindIT, Universitas Gadjah Mada) |

**Description.** Sleep apnea is a serious sleep disorder that affects sleep quality and increases the risk of long-term health problems, yet many people remain unaware of their condition due to limited awareness and early detection access. Appnea is an AI-powered sleep monitoring application that analyzes breathing sounds and patterns through a smartphone to help users identify potential sleep apnea risks. It offers an affordable, practical, and accessible solution without requiring additional medical devices.

**Vision.** Appnea envisions a future where people can take control of their sleep health through accessible and innovative technology. By combining artificial intelligence, sleep monitoring, and user-centered design, Appnea aims to encourage early awareness, support healthier sleep habits, and promote preventive healthcare.

**Goals.** Provide an easy-to-use platform that helps detect potential sleep apnea risks, increase awareness of the importance of sleep quality, and support early prevention of sleep-related health issues. Through AI-based analysis and personalized sleep insights, Appnea aims to reduce undiagnosed sleep apnea cases while creating a more inclusive and accessible approach to maintaining long-term health.

**Journey.** Sleep apnea is often a "silent threat" because many people experience symptoms without realizing the impact on their health. Fatigue, difficulty concentrating, and increased health risks can occur simply because sleep quality is disrupted. Through research and interviews with sleep apnea patients, individuals with related symptoms, and healthcare professionals, early detection was identified as a challenge. Many users struggle to recognize symptoms, find existing monitoring methods uncomfortable and expensive, and lack access to a simple solution that helps them understand their sleep condition. Appnea was developed with an empathetic approach, focusing not only on sleep apnea as a medical issue but also on how it affects daily activities, productivity, and quality of life. It aims to provide a non-invasive, intuitive, and accessible way to monitor sleep and become aware of potential risks.

**Problem and solution framing (from the project board).**
- Problems for the sleep apnea sufferer: often feels tired despite getting enough sleep, and is unaware of having sleep apnea until the condition becomes serious.
- Desired outcomes: a practical solution to detect sleep apnea early, and monitoring of sleep quality without additional tools.
- Existing solutions and their shortcomings:
  - Polysomnography (PSG): accurate but expensive and impractical.
  - CPAP machine: effective but uncomfortable for many people.
  - General sleep tracker: does not focus on detecting sleep apnea.

**Accessibility and design approach.** Appnea is designed to make sleep health monitoring accessible to a wider range of users. The application focuses on simplicity, comfort, and usability by allowing users to monitor sleep through smartphone-based sound and breathing analysis, without additional medical equipment. The interface uses clear information, easy navigation, and understandable insights so users can recognize sleep patterns without medical expertise. The visual identity uses a moon icon and purple gradient color. The moon represents nighttime, rest, and the natural sleep cycle, while the purple tone reflects calmness, relaxation, health, and innovation.

**Hope.** Through Appnea, the hope is to create a future where people are more aware of their sleep health and able to take preventive action earlier. By providing a practical and affordable sleep monitoring solution, Appnea aims to reduce undiagnosed sleep apnea cases and encourage healthier lifestyles. Beyond being a technology product, it represents a movement toward more inclusive healthcare, where everyone can understand their body, improve sleep quality, and achieve better well-being.

**Deliverables shown in the case study.**
- Customer journey map with four stages: Access, Choose, Share, Reserve (rows: touchpoints, actions, pain points, emotions, opportunities)
- User persona
- Empathy map (says, thinks, feels, does, pains, gains)
- Information architecture (sitemap)
- Low-fidelity wireframes
- Component library (navigation bars, buttons, chips, selectors, score rings showing sleep scores such as 85, emoji mood scale, gradient elements)
- High-fidelity mobile screens

**Design system.**
- Typeface: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Color: purple primary scale from blue-50 to blue-900 (token names retained from the Figma file)
- Brand mark: moon icon with cloud and purple gradient

**Key screens.** Home dashboard with sleep duration summary, heart rate detail view with daily, weekly, and monthly tabs and a heart rate chart, "Coba Appnea AI" entry point, and a "Butuh Bantuan Dokter?" (need a doctor?) prompt, plus add-data flow.

The approach focuses on simplicity, trust, and comfort through clear information, transparent pricing, and reliable recommendations. Research found that many job seekers with disabilities struggle to find matching opportunities, while companies struggle to identify suitable candidates. InCareer was developed to connect people with disabilities and inclusive companies, focusing on skills, qualifications, and accessibility, and simplifying the job search into three steps: choose, apply, and wait.
